# Script C (Short, about 48 seconds): the one problem isn't the reactor

**Format from:** H3, the "[person] solved the one problem stopping anyone from X" open loop that ran across five Shorts.
**On-screen title:** The ONE problem stopping a real Iron Man suit isn't the reactor
**Post caption:** Everyone blames the arc reactor. The numbers say otherwise. #ironman #engineering #physics #jetsuit
**Changes from the source:** the protagonist is a calculation you ran, not someone else's footage, so there is no copyright exposure, and "solved" becomes "I ran the numbers", which you can prove.

`|` marks where a caption ends. About 113 words, 2.4 words per second.

## Script

Everyone says the one problem stopping a real Iron Man suit | is the arc reactor. | I ran the numbers. | It's not. |
The real problem is hovering. | Hovering means pushing your whole weight down through the air, every second. | My suit burns about five kilograms of fuel a minute doing that. | Flying forward on a wing burns less than one. | That's five times less. |
So the fix isn't a better power source. | It's wings. | They swing back for speed, open wide to turn, | and tilt up to brake. |
With wings, the same suit flies for about an hour | when launched like a glider. | The reactor can wait. | Follow, and I'll show you the test.
