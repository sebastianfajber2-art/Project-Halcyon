# Script C (Short, about 47 seconds): the one problem isn't the reactor

Talking to camera, with three short film cutaways and your own overlays.

**Format from:** H3, the "[person] solved the one problem stopping anyone from X" open loop (see `../hooks.md`).
**Title on screen:** The ONE problem stopping a real Iron Man suit isn't the reactor
**Post caption:** Everyone blames the arc reactor. The numbers say otherwise. #ironman #engineering #physics #jetsuit
**Why it works as talking-to-camera:** the first line is a claim everyone has heard, the second line turns it over, and the film clips show the thing you're talking about while your face carries the argument. Nothing here claims a result you don't have: "I ran the numbers" is true.

## What you say

Read it as if you were telling a friend, not reading. About 113 words at 2.4 words per second.

> Everyone says the one problem stopping a real Iron Man suit is the arc reactor.
> I ran the numbers. It's not.
> The real problem is hovering. Hovering means pushing your whole weight down through the air, every second.
> My suit burns about five kilograms of fuel a minute doing that. Flying forward on a wing burns less than one. That's five times less.
> So the fix isn't a better power source. It's wings. They swing back for speed, open wide to turn, and tilt up to brake.
> With wings, the same suit flies for about an hour when launched like a glider. The reactor can wait.
> Follow, and I'll show you the test.

## Your own footage and overlays (nothing to license)

- **Shot 9:** film your hands as a wing swinging back, flat and tilting up, or use the concept sheet (`docs/brand/concept-sheet-a.png`) printed or on a screen, or a screen recording of the wing-mode diagram. When the foam wing is built, swap it in.
- **Overlays:** short words only, added in the edit, in the same blue and orange as the channel.
- **Captions:** white with a black outline at the bottom, one phrase at a time (the pause timing can be built from your audio).

## Filming

1. Phone vertical, at eye level, with the main light in front of you (a window or lamp), not behind.
2. Look at the lens, not the screen. Start talking within the first second: no "hey guys", no logo sting.
3. Say it in one take and do three. Leave the real pause before "It's not." and let it land.
4. If you're recording the camera through Iriun with the PC mic, do the clap test first so sound and picture line up.
5. Keep your energy a little higher than feels natural; a flat read looks slower on camera.
