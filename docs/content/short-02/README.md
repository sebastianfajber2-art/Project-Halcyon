# Short 2: "Can you build a real Iron Man suit?"

Vertical 9:16, 52.5 s, voiceover over clips (no face). Post to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. This is the explainer for new viewers; Short 1 (the arc reactor) is the shock hook, this one is the "what is this project" answer.

## Hook

Chosen: **"Can you build a real Iron Man suit? Yes. But not the way the movie does it."** (13 words, the answer and a twist in the first 5 seconds)

The original idea, "Are you a fan of Iron Man and are wondering if you can make a real Iron Man suit? Well, I just have the answer", takes about 9 seconds before any information, and most viewers have scrolled by then. It works fine as the opening of a long YouTube video, where people have already chosen to watch.

## Adding clips

The scene frames can be the spine of the video, with extra clips cut in on top for variety, roughly one every 4 to 6 seconds.

Use:
- **Your own screen recordings:** the Fusion model of the knee turning, the flight simulation output, the design document scrolling, the GitHub page.
- **Your own phone footage:** your desk, a 3D print, the €20 foam wing, hands testing a hinge.
- **Free stock** from Pexels or Pixabay: jet turbines, wingsuit flyers, workshops, sparks, circuit boards (check the licence on each clip).

Do not use Iron Man movie or trailer clips, even short ones: copyright claims can mute, demonetise or remove the video, and a strike on a new account is hard to recover from.
