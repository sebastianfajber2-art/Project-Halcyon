# Short 1: "Iron Man's arc reactor would cook him alive"

Vertical 9:16, ~40 s, voiceover only (no face). Post to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

## Files

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `timeline.json` | The edit timeline: 7 cuts (frame-exact at 30 fps), slow zoom per scene, 21 captions with style, music and sound cues. Feed this to an editor or editing MCP. |
| `captions.srt` | The same captions as a standard subtitle file |
| `voiceover.txt` | The read-aloud script to record (about 40 s, 88 words, 2.2 words per second) |
| `scene-01.png` … `scene-07.png` | The 1080 × 1920 visuals, one per scene (text is baked in) |
| `make_timeline.py`, `make_frames.py` | Generators: edit the scenes there and re-run, don't edit the outputs by hand |

Build order: record `voiceover.txt` → drop `voiceover.wav` next to the files → trim silences and re-time cuts and captions to the real voice → add music and sounds → export 1080 × 1920, 30 fps.

## Hook options

1. **Iron Man's arc reactor would cook him alive.** (chosen: shock + familiar character in 6 words)
2. Tony Stark would be dead in seconds, and here's the maths.
3. The arc reactor has one problem nobody talks about: heat.

## Edit notes

- Cut on every sentence; never hold one image longer than ~3 s without movement (slow zoom or pan).
- Captions: auto-captions on, large, centred in the middle third.
- Sound: low drone under scenes 1–5, a hit on "cook him alive" and on "three hundred million", music lifts at scene 6.
- Safe zones: all text sits between y = 300 and y = 1400, and nothing important is right of x = 940 between y = 950 and y = 1650, where the TikTok/Reels like, comment and share buttons sit.
- Frames are generated by `make_frames.py`; edit the text there and re-render instead of editing the PNGs.
- Caption for posting: "The arc reactor is impossible. So I'm building the real thing. #ironman #engineering #physics #jetsuit"

## Fact check

- 3 GJ per second (3 GW) is the figure Tony Stark quotes in the 2008 film.
- 10% of 3 GW = 300 MW; a resting human sheds roughly 100–300 W, a hard-working one up to about 1 kW, so the ratio is about 1,000,000× even before stress.
