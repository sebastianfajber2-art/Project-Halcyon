# Project Halcyon: social media and funding plan

Goal: fund Phase 0 and Phase 1 (about €3,000 for the first knee actuator, then €25,000) by building in public. Every claim stays physics-honest; that is the hook, not a weakness.

## Positioning

**"Building a real Iron Man suit, with real physics."** No fake arc reactor: every video shows the actual numbers, including what is impossible and why. Gravity Industries got its first investors from one viral prototype video; the realistic engineering angle is what separates this channel from cosplay builds.

## Platforms

| Platform | Format | Cadence | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube | 8–15 min build logs and explainers | Every 2 weeks | Main audience, sponsors, ad revenue later |
| YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels | 30–60 s clips cut from the long videos | 3 per week | Reach and growth |
| GitHub | This repository: sim, calcs, CAD | Every work session | Credibility with engineers and sponsors |
| Hackaday.io | Project page with build logs | Every milestone | Maker community, Hackaday Prize |
| Reddit (r/AerospaceEngineering, r/robotics, r/engineering) | Write-ups of results, not self-promotion | Per milestone | Expert feedback and reach |

## Funding routes, in order

| Route | When | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ko-fi or Patreon | From video 1 | Small monthly support; supporters get build files early and their names on the suit |
| GitHub Sponsors | Once the repo is public | Engineers fund open hardware |
| Component sponsorships | After ~5 videos with real results | Ask for parts, not cash: CubeMars, ODrive, mjbots, Bambu Lab, Autodesk Fusion, JetCat |
| Hackaday Prize and maker grants | Next open round | Fits the open-hardware exoskeleton work |
| Innovation or research grants, university partnership | Phase 1 onward | A university lab also brings test facilities and review |
| Crowdfunding | Only with a working prototype on video | Backers need to see hardware, not renders |

## Rules

- Show real tests and real failures; never fake results.
- Never show or encourage unsafe flight, untested gear on a person, or anything outside the test gates in the design document.
- No flying content until the suit is legally registered as an experimental aircraft.
- Put a safety disclaimer in every video description.
- Film every work session, even when nothing works: that footage becomes the failure videos.
